Methodology, sources & data audit

What the numbers are, where they come from, and what they cannot tell you.

Data audit · live check

Sources

  • Bond terms: grenke AG, Issued bonds overview (issuer, currency, issue size, coupon, dates, ESG label), plus the Final Terms, prospectus or pricing supplement linked there for each bond (Docs column).
  • FX: ECB euro reference rates of —, one rate per currency for every conversion. Local amounts are kept in the table.
  • Refresh: an automated job re-reads both sources every weekday and only replaces the bond list when the page parses cleanly.

Four different "costs"

Contractual couponThe fixed rate written in each bond. What this dashboard shows.
Coupon expenseNominal × coupon per year. Shown as "annual contractual coupon".
Effective funding costAdds issue price, fees and hedging. Not computable from this data.
Marginal refinancing costWhat a new bond would cost today. Approximated in the simulator, labelled as an assumption.

Definitions

  • Issued nominal: the issue size published by grenke. It is not a verified outstanding balance; buybacks, taps or partial redemptions would not show on that page.
  • Live: final maturity on or after today’s date; the perpetual AT1 counts as live. Matured bonds are excluded unless the Matured status filter is chosen.
  • Due ≤ 12 / 24 months, ≤ 5 years: issued nominal of live bonds whose final maturity falls between today and the end of the window.
  • Weighted average coupon: coupon rates weighted by EUR issued nominal.
  • Annual contractual coupon: issued nominal × coupon, in EUR. Every bond in scope pays a fixed coupon, so this is the yearly amount whether it is paid annually or semi-annually; payment dates and day-count conventions are not modelled.
  • Weighted average life: years to final maturity, weighted by EUR nominal; the AT1 is excluded.
  • Effective number of bonds: 1 ÷ the Herfindahl index (sum of squared nominal shares). It equals the bond count only if all bonds were the same size.

Simulator assumptions

  • Live bonds maturing by the chosen year are refinanced in the chosen share at the new-issue coupon, in EUR, for the same EUR amount; the remainder is repaid and stops paying interest.
  • Base new-issue coupon: nominal-weighted coupon of recent EUR senior issues. Favourable = base − 1.00 pp, adverse = base + 2.00 pp. These are assumptions, not market quotes.
  • Interest accrues by calendar year from each maturity date. Issue fees, discounts and hedging costs are ignored. The FX slider moves all non-EUR currencies by the same percentage against the EUR.

Limitations

  • Covers only the bonds on grenke’s public page. Deposits, ABS, bank facilities, commercial paper and other funding are outside the data, so nothing here measures total debt, liquidity or credit quality.
  • The AT1 coupon is discretionary and may reset at call dates; call dates are not modelled. Currency hedges are unknown and not reflected.
